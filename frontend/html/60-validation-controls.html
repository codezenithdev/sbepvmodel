Every validation run checks weather inputs before physics modeling. Short invalid wind or temperature gaps are repaired; longer gaps are omitted from both measured and predicted totals and reported with coverage.

System efficiencies

Enter decimal multipliers from 0 to 1. Inverter efficiency represents AC-conversion efficiency; balance of system covers other modeled losses. Solectria uses one common XGI MPPT from 860–1250 V, applies the efficiency multipliers, and remains capped at its 250 kW nameplate.

Use decimal values where 1.0 equals 100%.
