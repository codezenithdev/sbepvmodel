      LCOE and LCOO formulas

      LCOE = discounted lifecycle cost / discounted lifecycle AC energy.

      LCOO = (SolarEdge lifecycle cost − Solectria lifecycle cost) / (SolarEdge lifecycle AC energy − Solectria lifecycle AC energy).

      Commercial marginal LCOO = user-provided commercial marginal cost difference / consistently timed commercial energy difference scaled from the source-specific kWh per applied kW difference.

      LCOO is reported only where its energy-change denominator is valid under the calculation contract.
