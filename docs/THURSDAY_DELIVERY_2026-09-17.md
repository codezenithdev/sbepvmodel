### Interpretation limits

- Major-maintenance coverage remains unresolved. No separate charge was added; the lifecycle conclusion is provisional on the stated O&M assumptions.
- Later vendor/market prices remain unadjusted proxies within the proposed 2024-dollar scenario.
- Calibration excludes 14.683% of collected rows following the user's review and has limited fall coverage. The fit is not an independent validation dataset.
- The weather population contains 12 historical years. The report preserves eligibility rules and distinguishes cumulative percentiles from energy exceedance estimates.

### Why SolarEdge now has higher LCOE

Generic interface defaults assign both systems the same fixed $1.56/Wac CAPEX and $22/kWac-year O&M: $156 million and $2.2 million/year at 100 MWac, in 2022 dollars. The meeting preset uses different cost coverage and a proposed 2024-dollar basis. Its deterministic midpoints are $150.08 million plus $1.407 million/year for Solectria, versus $154.90915675 million plus $2.010 million/year for SolarEdge. The extra SolarEdge initial cost is $3.89115675 million of hardware plus $0.938 million of installation; its annual O&M is $603,000 higher.

The saved annual source also shows an energy ranking change: before calibration SolarEdge's aggregate predicted energy was 3.735% higher; after reviewed calibration it is 1.173% lower, and lower in every eligible weather year. Higher cost and lower calibrated energy both increase SolarEdge LCOE. This supports a mechanism for a physics-only comparison favoring SolarEdge, but the earlier default TEA is not in the local database, so its precise inputs cannot be confirmed. These scenarios are not an inflation-normalized comparison.
